#### 1. Steering Llama 2 via Contrastive Activation Addition

`2024-01-01` · **15** citations · _venue:_ ACL

> **Research question.** How can we steer language models by adding contrastive activation vectors to the residual stream, and how effective is this method?

**Core contribution.** Introduces Contrastive Activation Addition (CAA), a method for steering language models by adding contrastive activation vectors to the residual stream, which effectively steers behavior, works on top of finetuning and system prompts, and minimally reduces capabilities.

**Key findings.**
- CAA can effectively steer Llama 2 Chat models on a variety of alignment-relevant behaviors (AI Coordination, Corrigibility, Hallucination, Myopic Reward, Survival Instinct, Sycophancy, Refusal).
- CAA generalizes from multiple-choice to open-ended generation tasks.
- CAA can be combined with system prompting and finetuning to further control behavior.
- CAA has minimal impact on general capabilities as measured by MMLU.

_Target models:_ Llama 2 7B Chat, Llama 2 13B Chat, Llama 2 7B base
