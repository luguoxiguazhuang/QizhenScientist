#### 1. Toy Models of Superposition

`2022-09-21` · **37** citations · _venue:_ arXiv

> **Research question.** Why do neurons sometimes align with interpretable features and sometimes not, and how can neural networks represent more features than they have dimensions (superposition)?

**Core contribution.** The paper demonstrates that superposition—where neural networks store more sparse features than dimensions—is a real phenomenon, explains polysemanticity, and reveals a phase change and geometric structure in feature representations.

**Key findings.**
- Superposition is a real, observed phenomenon in neural networks.
- Both monosemantic and polysemantic neurons can form.
- At least some kinds of computation can be performed in superposition.
- Whether features are stored in superposition is governed by a phase change.

_Target models:_ ReLU networks, toy models
